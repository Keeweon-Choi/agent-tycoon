# Office UI

> Read before touching the canvas renderer, characters, sub-agent sprites, seats, bubbles, sound or UI styling rules.

## Office UI

**Rendering**: Game state in imperative `OfficeState` class (not React state). Pixel-perfect: zoom = integer device-pixels-per-sprite-pixel (1x–10x). No `ctx.scale(dpr)`. Default zoom = `Math.round(2 * devicePixelRatio)`. Z-sort all entities by Y. Pan via middle-mouse drag (`panRef`). **Camera follow**: `cameraFollowId` (separate from `selectedAgentId`) smoothly centers camera on the followed agent; set on agent click, cleared on deselection or manual pan.

**UI styling (FEAT-UI-MODERN-THEME, 11-06)**: two screen styles, one set of components. 설정 → 화면 스타일 (`agent-tycoon/uiTheme.ts`, one per browser, `agty.ui-theme`; the old 글꼴 key `agty.ui-font.v2` is read once: `pixel` stays 픽셀 감성, anything else becomes 모던) sets `data-ui-theme` on `<html>`. **모던** (default) is the GPT mockups' look (`specs/ui-mock-1104/hud-v3/base/gpt-*.png`): Pretendard Regular/Bold for all DOM text (`public/fonts/pretendard/`, `size-adjust: 70%` so the pixel-scale sizes draw at mockup size), windows 8 px / cards 6 px / chips 4 px / pills round, 1 px lines, soft drops (at most two layers) with a faint top highlight, a glow on the chosen item, Lucide outline icons, Material Symbols unfilled. **픽셀 감성** is the pixel UI as it was: FS Pixel Sans for ASCII, Galmuri11 for Hangul, sharp corners, 2 px lines, hard offset drops. The tokens live in `index.css`: `:root, .ui-world` holds the pixel values, `:root[data-ui-theme='modern']` the modern ones: `--ui-font`, `--font-data`, `--ui-weight-strong`, `--ui-title-scale`, `--ui-win-title`, `--ui-head-icon`, `--ui-bw`, `--ui-r-win/card/chip/pill`, `--ui-ease`, `--ui-glow`, `--ui-drop`, `--ui-notch-clip`, `--ui-text-floor` (the least a raw small px size draws at in modern), `--ui-text-render`, `--ui-ms-fill`, plus the UI-DEPTH `--bevel`, `--bevel-in`, `--shadow-pixel*` and `--head-step-*`. Tailwind utilities: `rounded-ui-win|card|chip|pill`, `border-ui` (and `-t/-b/-l/-r/-x/-y`). Icons: `<Icon name pixel>` (`agent-tycoon/icons/Icon.tsx`) draws the Lucide outline (`agent-tycoon/icons/lucide.ts`, a vendored subset, regenerate with `agent-tycoon/scripts/gen-lucide-subset.cjs`) in 모던 and `pixel` (the old glyph) in 픽셀 감성; `PixelIcon` and the HUD glyphs (`hud/glyphs.tsx`) go through it. The canvas keeps `pixelFontFamily()` (Galmuri11 ASCII in 모던, FS Pixel Sans in 픽셀 감성) and the office world's DOM parts carry `.ui-world` (the lobby billboard), so the world is pixel art in both. The character HUD over the canvas (the nameplate, the badges over and at the feet, the 휴식 bubble) is UI and follows the style (owner 11-06 18:14): in 모던 a rounded plate with a 1 px line, a soft drop, Pretendard (at least `--ui-text-floor`), a pill 피로도 bar and a state dot centred on the plate's left line, so the plate is no wider (`.ui-status-dot`, green working, yellow waiting, red permission, grey resting); the status, rank and level sprites stay pixel art; in 픽셀 감성 it is exactly as before. Every plate is one width per style and 화면 크기 step (T-67, `NAMEPLATE_SIZES.*.card`: what the owner's sample name needs, measured by `agent-tycoon/test/run-office-nameplate-fixed.ts MEASURE=1`), its second row `피로도 [fixed bar] 휴식 1/2`; a longer name ends in … and wraps whole inside the same width while hovered or selected. The plates take no pointer events: the canvas's hit test falls back to the plate under the pointer (`useNameplates.ts plateUnder`), so a hover over a plate shows its name and a click selects its character, while the wheel and drags stay the canvas's. Colours are the same palette in both (a few lines a touch lighter in 모던). **Depth (UI-DEPTH, 11-06)**: the shared parts are `.ui-*` classes in `index.css` over `:root` tokens (surfaces body < head < card < float, `--bevel`/`--bevel-in` = 98.css's raised/sunken inset pair, 1-4 px hard drops, a three-step `.ui-head`, the chosen one's two accent rings via `--ui-ring`, `.ui-notch` cut corners): `.ui-raise` lifts 1 px on hover and sinks on press (NES.css's flip), CSS only, 100 ms on transform and box-shadow, none under reduced motion or 게임 효과 off. Also `.ui-tabs`/`.ui-subtabs`/`.ui-tab`, `.ui-card` (+ `-tile`, `.ui-dots`, `.ui-status-dot`), `.ui-chip`, `.ui-badge`, `.ui-row`, `.ui-input`, `.ui-window`. `Button` variants: default, active, accent (주요), danger (위험), ghost (흐림), disabled.

**Buttons, rows, lists (UI-CONSISTENCY)**: every button in a panel, window or list is `components/ui/Button.tsx` (`sm` = h-28 for rows and panels, `md` = h-32 for a dialog's main action, `icon`; one height per size, text centred, one line, 44 px on touch), never a hand-styled `<button>`. Every 설정 row is `agent-tycoon/SettingRow.tsx` (title and help left, control right-aligned, `stacked` = full width under the text for three or more buttons or a field); a tab's panel sets the padding and the gap, a row never sets its own width. A list row (현황판, 팀원 popup, 스탯 view) is one line per field with `truncate` and the full text in `title`, its trailing button an `sm` Button centred on the row; the elevator's floor buttons, 편집 and 인테리어 share one width (`floors.css`). **PARTY-FRAME (11-04)**: the elevator is a 40px `▲ 1F ▼` strip on the left edge whose lamp opens that floor list beside it (remembered per device, `ELEVATOR_LIST_OPEN_STORAGE_KEY`); the party is a column of 56px portraits (`agent-tycoon/game/Party.tsx PartyPortrait`: face, state icon, +N temps, 8 context cells; work = purple border, waiting on the owner = a 기다림 tail, rest = dimmed face), on the desktop and, in a row, on the phone (FEAT-PARTY-V2 below; there are no full cards to unfold any more). `testHome.launchBrowser` opens the floor list for the runners written before it; `partyFrame: true` keeps the page default.

Custom ESLint rules (`eslint-rules/agent-tycoon-rules.mjs`) enforce: `no-inline-colors` (hex/rgb/rgba/hsl/hsla literals only in `constants.ts`), `pixel-shadow` (an inline box-shadow is a token, `var(--…)`, or the pixel `2px 2px 0px`), `pixel-font` (an inline font-family is a token, `var(--…)`, or FS Pixel Sans). All `error`-level — they block PRs.

**Characters**: FSM states — active (pathfind to seat, typing/reading animation by tool type), idle (wander randomly with BFS, return to seat after `wanderLimit` moves). 4-directional sprites, left = flipped right. Tool animations: typing (Write/Edit/Bash/Task) vs reading (Read/Grep/Glob/WebFetch). Sitting offset: characters shift down 6 px in TYPE state. Z-sort uses `ch.y + TILE_SIZE/2 + 0.5` so characters render in front of same-row furniture but behind lower-row furniture. **Chair z-sorting**: non-back chairs use `zY = (row+1)*TILE_SIZE` (capped to first row); back-facing chairs use `zY = (row+1)*TILE_SIZE + 1` so the chair back renders in front of the character. Chair tiles are blocked for all characters except their own assigned seat (per-character pathfinding via `withOwnSeatUnblocked`).

**Diverse palette assignment**: `pickDiversePalette()` counts palettes of current non-sub-agent characters; picks randomly from least-used palette(s). First `CHAR_COUNT` (23) agents each get a unique person; beyond that, people repeat with a random hue shift (45–315°) via `adjustSprite()`. Character stores `palette` (a built-in sheet number) + `hueShift` (degrees). A named agent's pinned look (characters.json, CHARS-1104) is also the least-used person; an employee's (`emp:<id>`) is its provider's mascot. Sprite cache keyed by `"palette:hueShift"`.

**Spawn/despawn effect**: Matrix-style digital rain animation (0.3 s). 16 vertical columns sweep top-to-bottom with staggered timing. Spawn: green rain reveals character pixels. Despawn: character pixels consumed by green rain trails. `matrixEffect` field on Character (`'spawn'`/`'despawn'`/`null`). Normal FSM is paused during effect. Restored agents (`existingAgents`) use `skipSpawnEffect: true` to appear instantly.

**Sub-agents**: Negative IDs (from -1 down). Created on `agentToolStart` with "Subtask:" prefix, or lazily by `subagentToolStart` when missing (watched background spawns, post-reload recreation). Start on the parent's palette, then wear their employee's look (a mascot), or with no employee yet the parent's provider's mascot (`applyLooks`, CHARS-1104). Click focuses parent terminal. Not persisted. Spawn at the closest free walkable tile to the parent (`closestFreeWalkableTile`) — around it, never in a seat. Idle (stop typing) when every tracked sub-tool row is done; overlay shows the latest non-done sub-tool status, falling back to the Subtask label.

**Speech bubbles**: Permission ("..." amber dots) stays until clicked/cleared. Waiting (green checkmark) auto-fades 2 s. Sprites in `spriteData.ts`.

**Sound notifications**: Ascending two-note chime (E5 → E6) via Web Audio API plays when waiting bubble appears (`agentStatus: 'waiting'`). `notificationSound.ts` manages AudioContext lifecycle; `unlockAudio()` on canvas mousedown resumes the context (webviews start suspended). Toggled via Settings modal. Persisted per-namespace in `<office folder>/config.json`.

**The owner's character (OWNER-AVATAR)**: `OfficeState.spawnOwner()` (from `useGameControls`' `ownerStep`, agty office only) puts the owner's own character (`OWNER_CHARACTER_ID`, `agtyOwner`) in front of the lobby board. It lives in `characters` so it walks, rides the elevator, draws and hit-tests as agents do; the agent-only paths skip it by `agtyOwner` (seats, wander, persisted seats, staff rules, chat in its menu, terminal focus). **Its 스탯 (OWNER-STATS)**: the menu's 스탯 opens `ez/OwnerStatCard.tsx` in the stats modal (`openStatDetail(OWNER_CHARACTER_ID)`): Lv, XP bar, today and total from `officeSnapshot.owner`. The daemon's `LevelStore` keeps the owner apart from agent rows (`levels.json` `owner`): XP from what a privileged `/ws` session got done (`levels.ts ownerActionOf`: a DM or room post, an allowed permission, a merge, judged by the answer; a Task added in `editTask`), and steps plus shown seconds from the page's `ownerActivity` report (`agent-tycoon/ownerStats.ts`, every 30 s), clamped to the time since the last accepted report. Its Lv up plays the level-up burst like an agent's (`noteRests`). Its look is `assignments.owner` (`looks.ts ownerChoice`, the lobby counter's rule; the daemon accepts the `owner` key). Input routing is `walk.ts walkRoute`: in the free camera the keys and joystick pan; otherwise they walk the selected character, else the owner's, which the camera follows whenever nothing is selected (a cleared selection, Esc, a camera mode picked, '내 캐릭터' in the free camera or a tap on it go back to it; on another floor it first rides there by `visitFloor`). **Seats**: Derived from chair furniture. `layoutToSeats()` creates a seat at every footprint tile of every chair. Multi-tile chairs produce multiple seats keyed `uid` / `uid:1` / `uid:2`. Facing direction priority: 1) chair `orientation` from catalog (front→DOWN, back→UP, left→LEFT, right→RIGHT), 2) adjacent desk direction, 3) forward (DOWN).
